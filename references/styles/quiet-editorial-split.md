# Quiet Editorial Split

Create one independent high-end editorial poster for each source photograph. The visual concept is a small subject surrounded by a large amount of empty space. The result should feel quiet, poetic, refined, minimal, innocent, relaxed, artistic, thoughtful, recognizable, and premium.

## Composition contract

- Produce one flat poster image in a strict 3:4 vertical aspect ratio.
- Split the canvas at the exact horizontal midpoint into two equal-height panels.
- Top panel: exactly 50% of the canvas.
- Bottom panel: exactly 50% of the canvas.
- Connect both panels through palette, mood, and narrative while maintaining a clear contrast between photography and illustration.
- Do not present the poster inside a frame, room, device, book mockup, or product mockup.

When exact pixel dimensions are accepted, prefer 1536 x 2048 or another exact 3:4 equivalent.

## Top panel: faithful source photograph

Use the original photograph as faithfully as the editing system permits. Preserve:

- subject identity and facial features;
- anatomy, body proportions, pose, gesture, and expression;
- clothing, important objects, and their relationships;
- primary composition, camera perspective, and spatial relationships;
- realistic texture, natural lighting, shadows, atmosphere, and original color mood.

Apply only restrained editorial color grading associated with premium magazines, contemporary art books, and independent publications. Keep the result photorealistic and authentic. Do not beautify, restyle, replace, or reconstruct the subject.

If fitting the format requires more image area, extend only sky, ground, walls, or other surrounding environmental background. Make the extension seamless and photographic. Never crop away, stretch, reshape, or distort the main subject merely to satisfy the format.

## Bottom panel: minimal handmade paper illustration

Reinterpret the photograph's most recognizable elements rather than reproducing every detail. Preserve the essential silhouette, proportions, pose or gesture, important objects, and core relationship between people and objects.

Render the illustrated subject small and centered, occupying about 10-20% of the bottom panel. Surround it with generous negative space. Suggest the environment with only a few lines or small shapes.

Use:

- delicate, slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines;
- a few bold, clearly defined acrylic-like flat color shapes;
- rough white, warm off-white, or pale natural paper stock;
- visible handmade brush marks;
- slightly irregular organic edges and subtle physical imperfections.

The illustration must remain immediately recognizable as a simplified interpretation of the top photograph. It should feel like a small handmade visual poem, not a polished digital cartoon.

## Palette

Extract the dominant colors from the source photograph and compress them into no more than four main colors. Keep the palette restrained, harmonious, and sophisticated. Use controlled flat color blocks while retaining paper grain and brush texture.

## Typography

Typography is optional. Add it only when it improves the composition. Keep it minimal and understated, such as a short neutral title, visible object name, or simple number.

Never invent a person's name, place, year, quotation, or other factual label. If suitable wording is not supplied by the user or clearly visible in the photograph, omit text. Use negative space deliberately and avoid display-advertising treatments.

## Exclusions

Do not use colored-pencil or crayon aesthetics, bleeding watercolor, pure line art, complex realistic illustration, heavy oil paint, smooth polished digital illustration, 3D rendering, glossy textures, commercial cartoons, cute mascot design, e-commerce styling, generic poster templates, excessive decoration, busy composition, or excessive typography.

Do not add borders between the panels unless a hairline is essential for clarity. Do not add extra photographs, repeated figures, invented objects, logos, watermarks, signatures, or explanatory captions.

## Prompt assembly

Build a compact editing prompt in this order:

1. State that the supplied photograph is the sole visual reference and one separate poster is required.
2. Summarize only the photograph-specific elements that must remain recognizable.
3. State the exact 3:4 canvas and 50/50 horizontal split.
4. Apply the top-panel fidelity requirements.
5. Apply the bottom-panel simplification, scale, negative-space, paper, and palette requirements.
6. Add typography only when user-supplied or clearly justified.
7. End with the most relevant failure exclusions: no collage, no subject distortion, no poster mockup, no commercial cartoon, no 3D, and no busy decoration.

## Acceptance checks

- Canvas is vertical 3:4.
- The horizontal division is centered: photograph 50% above and illustration 50% below.
- Exactly one source photograph is represented.
- The top panel preserves identity, anatomy, pose, clothing, objects, composition, and photographic realism.
- The bottom panel remains recognizable while using a small centered subject, broad negative space, handmade paper texture, and no more than four main colors.
- Any typography is sparse and contains no invented factual claims.
- The two halves read as one visual story and the overall work resembles an independent art-publication cover rather than an advertisement.
