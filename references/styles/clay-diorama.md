# Clay Diorama

## Intent

Reinterpret one photograph as a handcrafted clay miniature scene. Simplify forms enough to feel sculpted by hand while preserving the subject's identity, silhouette, pose, clothing colors, important objects, and emotional relationship.

## Format and composition

- Use a strict 4:5 vertical canvas.
- Stage one clear clay subject or small subject group on a compact tabletop set.
- Preserve the original action and spatial relationships. Reduce background detail to three to five supporting clay elements derived from the source.
- Use a slightly elevated or eye-level miniature-camera view. Keep the result as a flat poster image, not a photographed retail toy box.
- Leave a calm area for optional title text without placing lettering across a face.

## Material and light

- Use matte modeling clay, soft fingerprints, gentle tool marks, rounded handmade edges, and subtle variation in the material.
- Derive the palette from the source and compress it into five to eight principal colors.
- Use soft studio light, believable contact shadows, and shallow miniature depth without blurring the subject's face.
- Keep eyes, hair, clothing, and defining features simplified but recognizable. Avoid photorealistic skin pasted onto a clay body.

## Typography

- Use a short user-provided title or a neutral visible-subject label.
- Use simple rounded or hand-pressed lettering that feels made from the same clay system.
- Never invent names, brands, product claims, ages, dates, or packaging details.

## Acceptance checks

- The output is one flat 4:5 poster with a coherent handmade clay material.
- Subjects remain recognizable despite controlled simplification.
- Supporting props come from the source and do not overwhelm the scene.
- Lighting and shadows make the miniature physically believable.
- The result does not imply a commercial toy brand or product package.

## Avoid

Glossy plastic, vinyl-toy branding, retail blister packs, photoreal faces on toy bodies, deep 3D game rendering, excessive props, melted anatomy, duplicate limbs, unrelated cute accessories, heavy bokeh, and corporate mascot styling.
