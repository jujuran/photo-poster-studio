# Modern Ink Wash

## Intent

Reinterpret one photograph as a contemporary ink-wash poster with disciplined brush economy and expansive paper space. Preserve the source subject, gesture, silhouette, essential facial anchors, and environmental rhythm without turning it into a generic historical scene.

## Format and composition

- Use a strict 3:4 vertical canvas.
- Reserve roughly 35–60% of the canvas as breathing paper space.
- Establish the main subject with a small number of confident dark strokes, soft washes, and carefully preserved white gaps.
- Use atmospheric ink diffusion to simplify background depth while retaining recognizable mountains, plants, architecture, water, or room structure from the source.
- Do not add traditional buildings, costumes, cranes, mountains, calligraphy, or symbolic objects that are not present or requested.

## Ink, paper, and color

- Use carbon-black ink across a wide value range on warm white or pale natural fiber paper.
- Allow water blooms, dry-brush texture, feathered edges, and pooled ink only where they support form.
- One restrained mineral or botanical accent color is optional, sampled from the photograph.
- Protect faces and important objects from uncontrolled wash. Preserve identity through placement, contour, hair mass, and a few precise features.

## Typography

- Use user-provided text when available. Otherwise use one neutral title in a small serif or clean sans-serif, or omit text.
- Do not generate imitation calligraphy, artist signatures, poetry, seals, dates, or classical inscriptions without supplied wording.
- If the user supplies Chinese text, render it clearly and verify every character.

## Acceptance checks

- The result is one flat 3:4 poster with visible paper and intentional negative space.
- The subject remains recognizable through economical but accurate marks.
- Ink behavior feels physical rather than like a gray digital watercolor filter.
- Optional color remains subordinate to ink and paper.
- No invented cultural symbols, seals, signatures, or text appear.

## Avoid

Generic watercolor washes, full-color painting, crowded decorative borders, fake aged scrolls, gold foil, unreadable pseudo-calligraphy, random red seals, invented historical clothing, photographic cutouts pasted onto ink, over-detailed brushwork, and copied artist signatures.
