# Neon Future

## Intent

Turn one photograph into a credible near-future night poster. Preserve the source subject, identity, pose, vehicle or object geometry, and recognizable environment while introducing controlled luminous accents, atmospheric depth, and reflective color.

## Format and composition

- Use a strict 2:3 vertical canvas.
- Keep one clear photographic protagonist and one readable depth path through the scene.
- Preserve faces, body proportions, clothing, architecture, vehicles, and important objects.
- Extend only plausible darkness, haze, pavement, walls, sky, or existing light sources when the crop needs space.
- Do not replace an ordinary location with a megacity, spaceship, robot, or fantasy skyline unless requested.

## Light and color

- Use one primary neon accent and one secondary accent, supported by deep neutral shadows.
- Attach glow to believable light sources such as signs, edge strips, screens, stage lights, or reflections. Do not paint floating glow over the entire image.
- Use restrained haze, wet-surface reflection when plausible, and controlled bloom without obscuring faces or product details.
- Keep skin tones and material colors credible under mixed light.

## Typography

- Use a short user-provided title when available, with at most one small subtitle.
- Favor narrow geometric sans-serif or restrained monospaced type with clear spacing.
- Without supplied wording, use a neutral scene-supported title or omit text.
- Never invent corporation names, technical specifications, locations, dates, event details, warnings, or fictional-language glyphs.

## Acceptance checks

- The output is one flat 2:3 poster, not a billboard or screen mockup.
- The source remains recognizable beneath the future treatment.
- Every major glow has a plausible source and reflections follow scene geometry.
- Only two neon accent colors dominate.
- Text is readable and contains no invented factual claims or glyph noise.

## Avoid

Generic cyberpunk city replacement, excessive magenta-cyan wash, floating holograms without purpose, unreadable fake scripts, random circuitry, robots, spaceships, weapon additions, crushed facial detail, plastic skin, over-bloom, and game-cover clutter.
